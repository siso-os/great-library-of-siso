C-0001 — Rights and deletion architecture

STATUS: drafted  ·  BUDGET: one long lane, hard stop on overrun

QUESTION

How should rights, privacy and deletion state physically travel through the People Graph — from source observation, through canonical admission, into derived projections, embeddings, exports and backups — such that any source's removal obligation can be honoured and proven honoured?

WHY FRONTIER

  • Wrong is expensive: this is a legal and ethical failure mode, not a bug. Reddit's policy forbids retaining deleted data even de-identified; X requires deletion within 24h. Getting the cascade wrong means retaining data you were obliged to destroy.
  • Durable: a rights model outlives every schema version built on top of it.
  • Unblocks: every source pilot. You cannot ingest what you cannot delete. Cohort A's gate already says "no source proceeds to persistent storage until this harness passes."
  • Requires holding share-alike propagation, GDPR erasure, tombstones that survive rebuild, and cascade-into-embeddings in one head simultaneously. That is the discriminating skill.

The gap is not that nobody thought about this. It is that the thinking is entirely unimplemented. This commission closes the largest thinking-to-practice gap in the estate.

FALSIFIERS — what would prove the answer wrong

  1. A deletion drill leaves the record recoverable from any backup, snapshot, embedding, export or cached projection.
  2. Rights state cannot survive a common export — i.e. a CC BY-SA record loses its attribution requirement when projected.
  3. Honouring one source's obligation requires manual full-database surgery rather than a deterministic cascade.
  4. A share-alike-derived projection cannot be kept logically separable from CC0 metadata.
  5. The design requires a schema change so invasive that a rebuild from source is cheaper — in which case say so, and the answer is "rebuild", not "migrate".

DELIVERABLE

  • A concrete schema addition (DDL) covering rights, privacy classification, publication state and removal obligation at the levels the matrix requires: source, snapshot, observation, Work, evidence, claim, projection.
  • A purge cascade specification with a proof obligation — the exact query set that demonstrates a deleted record is gone from every derived surface.
  • A suppression-ledger design: non-content tombstones so historical snapshots cannot resurrect removed data.
  • A quarterly deletion-drill runbook, executable, one record per source class.
  • Land as a draft ADR in the Great Library plus DDL on a branch in siso-people-graph. Draft PR only — do not merge.

NOT IN SCOPE

  • Legal advice. Flag where counsel is required; do not substitute for it.
  • Ingesting any source. This is architecture, not acquisition.
  • Re-litigating the 38-source classifications — they were researched against primary terms.
  • Touching identity_v3, query_v3 or build_v3; other lanes own those.

WHY THIS IS FIRST

Every other source-expansion commission is gated behind it. The 180-day pilot portfolio's own Cohort A gate reads: "no source proceeds to persistent storage until this harness passes. If the current data model cannot represent source records separately from canonical decisions, stop and fix that seam first." That seam is this commission.
