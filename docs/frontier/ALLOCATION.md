# Allocate to the next useful investigation

This is a proposed decision procedure, not a canonical portfolio ranking, measured
return on investment or authorization to spend. Question wording and states stay
in the Library. Allocation belongs to the human/domain decision owner.

## Importance and marginal value are different

Strategic importance asks what a better answer could change over the relevant
horizon. Marginal research value asks what the **next bounded investigation** can
change relative to the best decision available now. A large long-term question
can have a low-value next probe; a small urgent uncertainty can deserve priority.

For each candidate batch, record the affected decision and its owner, deadline,
current baseline, alternative choices, uncertainty most likely to change the
choice, cheapest discriminating test, shared dependencies, full effort bounds,
and failure/renewal conditions. Keep source-reported estimates, assumptions and
measured outcomes separate. Use low/base/high ranges where a value estimate is
useful, and preserve the basis and date. Do not turn an ordinal score into money.

A conceptual comparison is expected outcome with evidence minus expected outcome
under the best current decision minus acquisition, inference, review, repair and
attention costs. This is a framing aid, not a formula with empirically known inputs.
Where common units are unjustified, keep a cost vector and compare alternatives
under explicit constraints. Unknown cost does not mean zero or unlimited access.

## Decision process

First remove proposals without an actual consumer, lawful evidence access, a
bounded scope, usable accounting or a stop path. Next compare the smallest probes
for the remaining decisions, including keeping the current choice. Prefer probes
that distinguish live alternatives and unblock other useful work. Give shared
infrastructure only its marginal contribution; never allocate its entire benefit
again to every dependent question.

Fund one batch at a time. Keep explicit unallocated capacity for verification,
failed attempts and a competing hypothesis. A negative or no-change result earns
credit when it resolves a predeclared consequential uncertainty. An unchanged choice
with no new evidence and no reduced effort does not earn a positive outcome merely
because an agent wrote a confident report.

Renew only with a remaining decision-relevant uncertainty, a credible next test,
updated full-cost evidence and an owner who can use the return. Stop or redirect
when additional evidence ceases to discriminate, real-world integration is the
missing test, verification consumes the claimed benefit, or the owner cannot act.
No refresh trigger silently renews its own budget.

## Observation is not permanent expensive reasoning

A sustained system should normally use deterministic collection, caching and delta
checks through Foundry, and invoke reasoning for a material change or approaching
decision. Keep observations comparable in time and preserve query/coverage limits.
Retries and failed acquisition count against the envelope. The operating owner must
establish the source's current API/terms limits before enabling any collector.

Do not install a second GitHub scraper or People Graph. For GQ-014, start with the
existing Foundry candidates tied to a real capability. Compare fit, release and
review behavior, maintenance concentration, rights, integration and removal costs.
Repository ownership, contribution, review responsibility and package publication
are separate roles, not interchangeable maintainer labels. Popularity is discovery
signal, never adoption proof.

A useful initial *proposal* is 20 candidate reads, at most five deeper dossiers and
two pending adoption/retention decisions. These are batch ceilings, not an optimal
sample size or a mandate. Freeze the owner's baseline before the research and
record both changed decisions and justified no-change outcomes. Count costs once
while linking the return to GQ-014, GQ-004, GQ-010 and GQ-023 as appropriate.

## Minimal evaluation record

Record accepted/rejected/inconclusive outcome in the owning task, with the relevant
receipt references. Measure source coverage and provenance separately from decision
quality; input/output tokens separately from actual account charge; elapsed time
separately from human attention; runtime compatibility separately from model quality.
Report missing measurements and confounders. A structural pass, agent count, token
volume, row count or polished page is not a productivity result.
